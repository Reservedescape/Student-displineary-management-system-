Help & Documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions regarding system rules, hearing schedules, and sanction rulings.

Why is the Sanction Module locked until the hearing date?
SDMS enforces strict procedural due process. Disciplinary sanctions and rulings can only be issued AFTER a hearing has been scheduled and conducted. Locking the sanction module until the hearing date guarantees that students receive a fair hearing before any penalty is imposed.
How does hearing schedule persistence prevent duplicate entries?
When committee staff update or reschedule a hearing, SDMS updates the existing database record for that case rather than inserting duplicate hearing rows. Additionally, the system automatically sorts entries by latest ID to maintain single-record consistency.
How can a student file an appeal against a ruling?
After a sanction is recorded by the committee, students can open their case card in the Student Portal and click "Submit Appeal". They can provide written grounds for appeal and attach supporting documentation for administrative board review.
What happens after an administrator reviews an appeal?
Administrators can approve the appeal (overturning or modifying the sanction), deny the appeal (maintaining the ruling), or remand the case back for a rehearing. All parties receive automated real-time status updates via Supabase.
